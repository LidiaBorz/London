Task 1. Fill in the blanks

1. Look outside! It (...) with rain. [pour]
2. We couldn't go to the beach yesterday because it (...) all day. [rain + сильно]
3. The forecast says the weather (...) later today. [deteriorate]
4. Listen! A strong wind (...) outside. [blow]
5. The storm was terrible. Several rivers (...). [burst their banks]
6. Don't go out without a coat — it (...) today. [be + ледяной холод]
7. Yesterday freak weather conditions (...) the south-west. [hit]

Task 8. Complete the dialogue

Заполните пропуски в диалоге, используя изученные фразы. Форма глаголов может меняться.

A: Oh no! Did you get wet?
B: We (...) on the first day. [get soaked]
And in the tent it was (...). [freezing cold]
A: What a nightmare! And what about the weather now?
B: Well, the forecast says it (...) later today. [deteriorate]
A: Look outside — it certainly (...). [look like rain]
B: Yes, and the wind (...) too. [pick up]
A: Did you hear about the storm in the south-west?
